## paints.json

Converted from `data/paints/warhammer.json` in [Paintdex](https://github.com/s10-steve/paintdex) by `tools/build-paints.py`. Only id, name, range, type, colour, discontinued and metallic are kept. Colours are approximate. Paintdex's paint list was originally imported from [Miniature Painter Pro's paint list](https://github.com/Arcturus5404/miniature-paints).

Warhammer Colour (formerly Citadel Colour) paint names are trademarks of Games Workshop. This app is not affiliated with or endorsed by Games Workshop or any paint manufacturer.
